## 📂 Detailed Activity Breakdown & Reflections (Activities 1–7)

### 🐱 Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles (Free Coffee Poster)
* **Folder:** [`./Activity-1`](./Activity-1) | **Canva Output:** [View Activity 1 Design](https://canva.link/yfh509fb8r5la0d)
* **Description:** This activity focused on applying core visual design principles—specifically visual hierarchy, contrast, balance, whitespace, and proximity—to design an engaging promotional poster titled *"Free Coffee"*.
* **What Have I Learned?**
  * **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to guide a viewer's eye intentionally using typography scale and contrast—pairing a flowing script font for *"Free"* with a bold serif font for *"Coffee"*, and placing dark green typography against a light green background for strong readability. I also practiced grouping related details like the booth location, date, and *"first come, first serve"* notice using proximity.
  * **Challenge & Solution:** My main challenge was keeping the poster energetic and eye-catching without overcrowding the layout with text. I solved this by establishing a clear top-to-bottom visual flow—placing the main headline at the top, the coffee illustration in the center, and concise supporting text at the bottom with generous whitespace.
  * **Future Application:** In my future nursing practice, I can apply these exact layout and hierarchy principles when creating patient health education posters and community clinic advisories so critical health instructions stand out immediately.

---

### 🎨 Activity 2: Color Palette & Typography (Personal Visual Branding System)
* **Folder:** [`./Activity-2`](./Activity-2) | **Canva Output:** [View Activity 2 Brand Guide](https://canva.link/0id0zp34jj2poyk)
* **Description:** This activity involved developing a cohesive personal visual branding system—complete with a coordinated color palette, three-tier typography hierarchy, movement-inspired logo, personal tagline (*"Move, Breathe, and Flow"*), and branded resume header.
* **What Have I Learned?**
  * **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I gained a deeper understanding of color psychology, contrast ratios, and functional typography pairing—combining vivid red (`#9B111E`), deep dark red (`#5A0A12`), soft red (`#C93B4B`), warm cream, and charcoal black with display serif headings, script accents, and clean sans-serif body text.
  * **Challenge & Solution:** Choosing colors and fonts that looked attractive individually while still feeling unified across different formats (a brand board, logo, and resume header) was initially challenging. I overcame this by assigning strict roles to each color and typeface—using cream as a clean canvas, dark tones for contrast, and red for focal emphasis.
  * **Future Application:** Having an established, accessible visual identity system gives me a consistent aesthetic foundation that I can carry forward into my professional nursing portfolio, digital resume, and future personal projects.

---

### 🏥 Activity 3: Social Media Infographic & Project Documentation (SMARTQUEUE)
* **Folder:** [`./Activity-3`](./Activity-3) | **Canva Infographic:** [View SMARTQUEUE Infographic](https://canva.link/ft0pss9hvbvjv8s) | **Project Documentation:** [View SMARTQUEUE Proposal](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-HbU1OsJmLyMwxQYcWw0hbkXryjBIhQs98ZUlK5C6pA/edit?usp=sharing)
* **Description:** This activity paired a formal written project proposal with a one-page infographic introducing **SMARTQUEUE**, a digital patient queue management system designed to reduce hospital waiting times, overcrowding, and staff administrative workload.
* **What Have I Learned?**
  * **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to translate a detailed healthcare technology proposal into a scannable visual narrative by structuring the infographic into logical sections (the problem, why it matters, key features, before-and-after comparison, and expected impact) using a cream, red, black, and gray palette.
  * **Challenge & Solution:** Condensing technical features—such as automated registration, real-time queue displays, priority triage flagging, and wait-time analytics—onto a single infographic without overwhelming the reader was difficult. I solved this by writing the full documentation first, extracting only the highest-impact points, and pairing them with healthcare icons and a side-by-side comparison.
  * **Future Application:** As a future nurse, I can use this combination of structured project documentation and visual storytelling to propose clinical workflow improvements and communicate quality-improvement initiatives clearly to hospital administrators and multidisciplinary teams.

### 👗 Activity 5: AI Image Generation & Iterative Prompt Engineering
* **Folder:** [`./Activity-5`](./Activity-5) | **Google Docs Output:** [View Activity 5 Documentation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1R_kthPl52IE3BS4uopvzFGoABXS80Km_DWO3Kr0qnDU/edit?usp=sharing)
* **Description:** This activity explored iterative AI prompt engineering by conceptualizing and refining a Victoria's Secret–inspired runway Halloween costume—evolving an initial ethereal white-and-silver design into a regal, modest red-and-gold Angel ensemble.
* **What Have I Learned?**
  * **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how specific prompt modifiers—covering color symbolism (red for confidence and strength, gold for radiance), silhouette adjustments, neckline modesty, and fabric detailing—directly shape generative AI visual outputs.
  * **Challenge & Solution:** The initial AI output produced a low neckline and high slit that felt too revealing compared to my intended aesthetic. I solved this through iterative refinement—writing a follow-up prompt that specified a richer red-and-gold palette alongside a higher, softly square neckline and floor-length skirt with a minimal slit.
  * **Future Application:** Mastering iterative prompt refinement enables me to generate tailored, culturally appropriate visual aids and custom illustrations for health education materials rather than settling for generic first-draft AI results.

### 🧘‍♀️ Activity 6: AI-Assisted UI/UX & Website Blueprint (Luma Pilates Studio)
* **Folder:** [`./Activity-6`](./Activity-6) | **Google Docs Output:** [View Activity 6 Documentation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Oa3JKTXp0eF1Jg5vyEdTQq3bjipsX5Lwzf308tVgqFA/edit?usp=sharing)
* **Description:** This activity involved engineering a comprehensive, structured AI prompt and design blueprint for **Luma Pilates Studio**, a modern boutique wellness website featuring brand identity guidelines, service filtering, booking workflows, membership tiers, and ethical UX guardrails.
* **What Have I Learned?**
  * **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to translate a business concept into a complete UI/UX specification—defining target demographics, an editorial color palette (`#F8F5F0` warm ivory, `#A8B5A0` sage green, `#424B3F` deep olive), multi-page architecture, accessibility standards, and transparent booking/pricing disclaimers.
  * **Challenge & Solution:** Designing a conversion-focused wellness site that felt luxurious without relying on exaggerated fitness claims or fake booking availability was a major consideration. I addressed this by building explicit ethical guardrails into the prompt—requiring placeholder labels, honest front-end booking disclosures, and responsible, evidence-aligned wellness language.
  * **Future Application:** Understanding user-centered web architecture, accessibility, and ethical digital health communication will help me evaluate and contribute to patient-facing telehealth platforms and clinic portals in my nursing career.

### 📄 Activity 7: AI-Assisted Resume Creation & Comparative LLM Analysis
* **Folder:** [`./Activity-7`](./Activity-7) | **Google Docs Output:** [View Activity 7 Documentation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zolbaFHHR6t2flXuzIx9FwfpeeEjqcrkOY9qgfGhcXg/edit?usp=sharing)
* **Description:** This activity involved using two Large Language Models (ChatGPT and Gemini) to generate a professional nursing resume, conducting a structured side-by-side comparison of their formatting and content coverage, and synthesizing a polished final resume.
* **What Have I Learned?**
  * **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how different LLMs prioritize structure versus role-specific framing—observing how ChatGPT produced a modern sans-serif layout with a nursing-targeted summary, while Gemini generated a traditional, comprehensive outline covering all four clinical rotations (Maternal & Child, Medical-Surgical, Community Health, and Nursing Leadership), education, BLS certification, and core skills.
  * **Challenge & Solution:** Neither AI output was completely ready on its own—one truncated sections visually while the other opened with a generic summary rather than immediately highlighting my BS Nursing background. I solved this by critically evaluating both outputs in a comparison table and combining the strongest elements of each into a cohesive final resume.
  * **Future Application:** Critically auditing and cross-checking AI outputs rather than copying them blindly is an essential 21st-century skill that I will apply when preparing job applications, clinical portfolios, and evidence-based healthcare summaries.
