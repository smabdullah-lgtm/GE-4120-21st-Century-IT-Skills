## 🐾 About Me

Hello! I am **Shanara M. Abdullah**, a 4th-year **Bachelor of Science in Nursing** student (Section 4D) at **Ateneo de Davao University** in Davao City, Philippines. Alongside my clinical training in maternal-child, medical-surgical, community health, and nursing leadership, I am passionate about aesthetic visual design, digital content creation, healthcare innovation, and wellness. 

This repository serves as my centralized digital portfolio for **GE 4120: 21st Century IT Skills**, highlighting my creative and technical growth from Preliminaries through Finals.

### 🛠️ Skills & Tools Learned
* **Visual & Graphic Design:** Canva, Visual Hierarchy, Typography Pairing, Color Theory & Accessibility, Personal Brand Identity Systems
* **Healthcare & Technical Documentation:** Project Proposal Writing, Data Visualization, Healthcare Workflow Design (SMARTQUEUE)
* **AI & Prompt Engineering:** ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Iterative Prompt Refinement, AI-Assisted UI/UX Web Blueprinting, Comparative Output Evaluation
* **Version Control & Digital Organization:** GitHub Repository Architecture, Markdown Documentation, Structured File Management

#### 🍵 Overall Course Reflection & Key Takeaways 🐾

</div>

> **GE 4120: 21st Century IT Skills** transformed how I view technology—not just as a set of digital tools, but as a bridge between creativity, clear communication, and real-world problem-solving. 
>
> Throughout the term, I learned that great ideas only make an impact when they are presented with intention. In the **Preliminaries**, mastering visual hierarchy, typography, and cohesive personal branding taught me how design shapes first impressions and makes complex information accessible—a lesson I applied directly to healthcare when designing **SMARTQUEUE** to address hospital waiting times. Moving into the **Midterms and Prefinals**, exploring iterative prompt engineering, UI/UX website architecture, and AI-assisted resume building showed me how to collaborate critically with artificial intelligence rather than relying on it passively. 
>
> As a **BS Nursing** student preparing for clinical practice and future career opportunities, these 21st-century digital skills have equipped me to communicate health education more clearly, organize digital workflows efficiently, and present my professional identity with confidence, clarity, and purpose. 🐈‍⬛🍵
